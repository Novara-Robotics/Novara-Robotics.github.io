How Deri works

One workflow from early warning to resolution.

Deri connects live equipment data with manuals, work orders, maintenance history, and past incidents, helping teams detect issues earlier and recover faster.

  1. 01

    Monitor

    Detect anomalies, changing equipment behavior, and emerging failure patterns.

  2. 02

    Investigate

    Assemble the signals, alarms, documents, and past incidents relevant to the issue.

  3. 03

    Resolve

    Rank likely causes, recommend next steps, and guide technicians through recovery.

  4. 04

    Learn

    Record what worked so future incidents begin with more context.

Operational outcomes

Results you can measure.

Each pilot starts with a baseline and tracks the metrics tied to the selected workflow.

Early-warning lead time increase

Time between an emerging issue being detected and the resulting intervention or failure.

Time to diagnosis decrease

Time from the first alert to a likely cause and recommended next action.

Mean time to repair decrease

Time from an equipment incident to restored production.

Unplanned downtime decrease

Total production time lost across the selected equipment or workflow.

Depending on the workflow, we also track time spent retrieving manuals and prior context, documenting incidents, and completing work orders.
