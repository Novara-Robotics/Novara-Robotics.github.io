The AI reliability engineer for factories.

Better visibility. Faster response. Less downtime.

How Deri works

From early warning to resolution.

Deri detects emerging equipment issues, investigates failures, and guides teams from alarm to resolution across machines, robots, and production systems.

  1. 01

    Monitor

    Detect anomalies, changing equipment behavior, and emerging failure patterns.

  2. 02

    Investigate

    Bring together the signals, alarms, documents, and past incidents relevant to the issue.

  3. 03

    Resolve

    Rank likely causes, recommend next steps, and guide technicians through recovery.

  4. 04

    Learn

    Capture the resolution so future incidents begin with more context.

Deployment

Built to fit your factory, not disrupt it.

Start narrow

Begin with one equipment group, recurring incident, or maintenance workflow.

Work with existing systems

Run alongside current processes or connect to selected PLCs, robots, sensors, historians, MES, CMMS, and maintenance records.

Deploy your way

Choose cloud, hybrid, or on-premises deployment based on your plant requirements.

Stay in control

Start in read-only mode and keep data access, permissions, and operational actions under customer-defined controls and human approval.

We handle the rollout.
