DERI

The AI reliability engineer for factories.

better visibility. faster response. less downtime.

Deri monitors equipment, detects emerging issues, investigates failures, guides recovery, and learns from every incident across machines, robots, and production systems.

How Deri works

From early warning to verified resolution.

Deri brings live equipment signals, alarms, manuals, work orders, maintenance history, previous incidents, and technician knowledge into one reliability workflow.

  1. 01

    Monitor

    Deri watches machine and process signals, alarms, and operational events to surface anomalies, changing equipment behavior, and emerging failure patterns.

    • Machine and robot telemetry
    • PLC alarms and events
    • Sensor data and process variables
    • Equipment states and operating patterns
  2. 02

    Investigate

    When an issue appears, Deri automatically assembles the relevant evidence from live machine states, recent changes, manuals, SOPs, work orders, maintenance history, and similar past incidents.

    • Ranks likely causes
    • Shows supporting evidence
    • Does not state root cause as certainty
  3. 03

    Resolve

    Deri recommends the next diagnostic checks, guides technicians through recovery, coordinates escalation, and supports approved operational actions within customer-defined permissions.

    • Recommended next steps and questions
    • Manuals, procedures, and similar incidents
    • Incident and work-order updates
    • Escalation recommendations
  4. 04

    Learn

    After the issue is resolved, Deri records the symptoms, actions, causes, and outcomes so the next response begins with more context.

    • Structured incident history
    • Searchable repair knowledge
    • Better shift handovers
    • Recurring-failure identification

Deployment

Built to fit your factory, not disrupt it.

Start with one workflow or equipment group, connect only the systems required, and expand after value has been demonstrated.

Start narrow

Begin with one recurring incident, selected asset group, or reliability workflow instead of attempting a plant-wide rollout on day one.

  • Focused initial scope and defined operational workflow
  • Selected machines or production systems
  • Agreed success criteria before expansion

Work with existing systems

Deri can operate alongside current workflows or connect with selected factory systems, including PLCs, robots, sensors, historians, MES, CMMS, manuals, SOPs, and maintenance records.

  • No rip-and-replace requirement
  • Standalone, parallel, or integrated deployment
  • Connect only the systems needed for the initial workflow
  • Existing systems remain systems of record where appropriate

Deploy your way

Run Deri in the cloud, in a hybrid configuration, or on-premises using the Novara OT Box, depending on plant requirements.

  • Cloud, hybrid, or on-premises architecture
  • Restricted-network or air-gapped environments where technically feasible
  • Customer-selected deployment model

Stay in control

Deployments can begin in read-only mode. Data access, user permissions, and operational actions remain governed by customer-defined controls and human approvals.

  • Read-only starting mode
  • Role-based permissions and customer-controlled access
  • Human approval for operational actions
  • Evidence shown behind recommendations
  • Auditability of incident actions and outcomes

Novara handles the rollout.

Novara works with your operations, maintenance, controls, and IT or OT teams to configure the workflow, map the relevant data, integrate selected systems, train users, and support the deployment.

  1. Select the workflow and success metrics
  2. Identify the required systems and data
  3. Configure integrations and operational context
  4. Train the relevant users
  5. Launch the focused deployment
  6. Review results and decide whether to expand

Focused deployments are scoped around the selected systems, workflow, and plant requirements. The initial deployment is designed to minimize customer effort and avoid unnecessary operational disruption.
